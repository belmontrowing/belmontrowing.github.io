YOUTH TEAM

Our Youth Team is for middle and high school youth (starting ~12 years old) who have some rowing experience. Inexperienced rowers will join our Youth Team after completing a Learn to Row Camp. The Youth Team is a great alternative to school-based athletic programs, where you can engage in athletics, be part of a inclusive team, and develop a skill that can be leveraged in college and throughout life. At BRC, there are no tryouts or cuts to make the team. The more athletes we have, the more opportunities we can provide to our community.

Join our Youth Rowing Charlotte area team today or at any time during the season and become part of a vibrant community!

Special exceptions can be requested using our contact form.

Financial Aid Available

We offer financial aid for programs based on need. These scholarships are limited by fund availability. To learn more contact us here.

2026 Youth Team - Fall Season

Register

The 2026 Youth Fall Season starts on August 17, 2026. For the Fall, BRC is offering programming for under 19 year-olds (U19), under 17 year-olds (U17) and under 13 year-olds (U13), with more frequent rowing days for the older and more experienced youth rowers. The season consists of on-the-water practices (weather permitting) on Mondays, Tuesdays, Wednesdays, Fridays and Saturdays (U19 – M/T/W/F/S, U17 – M/T/F/S, and U13 – F/S). There is a weekly, optional but recommended, strength training session at the Riverside Athletic Club, across from Belmont Rowing Center. Our Youth Parent Liaison(s) will help organize directly with Coach Q. Fees are paid up-front for the season or on a monthly payment schedule through our club management system – iCrew. The fee for the Fall season is $1,250 (U19), $1,100 (U17), and $550 (U13). Fee/costs for Regattas and Strength Training are handled and paid separately.

Rowing is a great team-oriented sport that is low-impact, provides a full-body workout, and teaches focus & drive. Each of our summer camps teaches the fundamentals of rowing, understanding that campers have probably never touched an oar before. Experienced members of our youth team assist the learn to row camps, mentoring new rowers. Rowers should be at least 12 years old or in 6th grade

2026 Summer Camp Offerings

Learn-To-Row:

For Youth age 12 and older, these sessions will focus on teaching the fundamentals of rowing techniques, boat handling, and water safety. The camp provides a safe, supportive environment where students can develop their skills, build teamwork, and enhance their physical fitness. This program not only introduces the sport, but also encourages a love for outdoor activities and nurtures a sense of accomplishment among the young athletes. Click on below links to register.

Monday June 15 – Friday June 19, sessions run from 8-10AM ($200)

Monday August 3 – Friday August 7, sessions run from 8-10AM ($200)

Development Program:

This is a more specialized program designed to refine LTR and experienced Youth’s skills and push their limits. The camps focus on advanced rowing techniques, race strategy, and endurance training. Participants will engage in more rigorous on-water practices, complemented by strength conditioning and technical drills to enhance their overall performance. The program also includes video analysis sessions to help rowers analyze their technique and make precise adjustments. Ideal for those who have previously rowed at BRC or want to row in the fall season, these camps aim to prepare young rowes for competitive rowing, fostering both individual prowess and team cohesion (and we’ll also have fun!).

Monday June 22 – Friday June 26, sessions run from 8-10AM ($200)

Monday August 10 – Friday August 12, sessions run from 8-10AM ($120)
